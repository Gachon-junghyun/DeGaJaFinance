# PROTOCOL — 미러링 (mirror)

> A protocol = an ordered composition of L1 blocks. **Order is owned by this file.** L1s are referenced only.
> Purpose: mirror the **real KIS account** into the paper book (so judgment is made against reality), apply the
> research desks' judgment, and **stage the recommendations onto the real KIS order desk** as human-fireable cards.
> Engine = `module_paper_book` (+`module_order_desk` stack, `module_epistemics` vault). Output root
> `llm_outputs/{YYYY-MM-DD}/미러링/`. Runtime `--market us` (미국 지식 한정 — US-knowledge-limited by default).

## What this desk is (and is NOT)
- **IS:** the bridge from research → the *real* book. It ingests the actual KIS holdings, decides against them,
  and drops the resulting intent cards on the KIS order desk stack (`out/order_desk/kis_stack.json`).
- **IS NOT:** an executor. Every card is dry-run; **a human fires each with [체결]** (P5). No `--execute`, no
  broker call, no auto-fire. Recommendations are analytical/illustrative — **not licensed financial advice**.
- **Judgment basis (epistemics):** DECIDE consults `epistemics/sensitivity/{ticker}` (learned factor
  elasticities/confidence) as a prior and writes today's factors back — the vault at `epistemics/sensitivity/`
  is where that knowledge accumulates and compounds across runs.

## File-output rules
- Run outputs → `llm_outputs/{date}/미러링/`: `MIRROR_STATE.md` · `DECISIONS.md` · `ORDERS.md` · `STAGED_ORDERS.md`.
- State-of-record files (mutated in place): `data/paper_book.db` (the mirrored+applied book) ·
  `out/order_desk/kis_stack.json` (the staged cards) · `epistemics/sensitivity/*` (accumulated basis).
- No secrets in any output. Every derived number is module-computed.

## Runtime deltas (vs paper_desk)
- **Book source = the real KIS account, not a fantasy capital.** MIRROR_INGEST replaces the paper positions with
  reality first; every later stage reasons against what is actually held (and its concentration).
- **US-knowledge-limited (미국 지식 한정):** mirror the US sleeve; a KR holding is noted but out of scope this desk.
- **STAGE_ORDERS is the terminal action** (paper_desk ends at REVIEW; 미러링 ends by staging to the *real* desk) —
  and it stages intents only. The human executes; the desk never does.
- **The mirror is the source of truth** — only MIRROR_INGEST may reset the book to the real holdings.

**Start → read [MIRROR_INGEST](../L1_stages/mirror_ingest.md) and execute.** Advance only after each EXIT CHECK
passes. Finish with [handoff](../handoff.md) so the paper journal folds into the tag ledger.
