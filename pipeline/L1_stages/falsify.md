# L1 · FALSIFY — refutation pass (stage)

> Phase D-refute (main thread, right before VERDICT). The desk's job is to *break* the thesis, not defend it.
> A verdict that survived a genuine bear case is worth money; one that was never attacked is narrative.
> Reuses the PLAY43 thinking-card library. Output: the refutation section of REPORT.

## What this stage does
1. **Write the strongest bear case FIRST** — if this company were fake or over-hyped, *where* in Block A
   (product/bottleneck/moat) or Block B (accruals/money-trail) would the crack show? State it as the bear
   would, at full strength — no strawman.
2. **Reject or uphold each bear item** with the A/B/D evidence already gathered. An item you cannot reject
   stays *upheld* and must dent the verdict — do not wave it away.
3. **One-to-two thinking cards, and at least one must be a refutation hammer** (PLAY43 library,
   `08_thinking_functions_execution.md`). Use the card to *break* the thesis, never to manufacture it.

## Guards
- No hedging to protect a prior verdict. If the bear case wins, say so and route the verdict toward INFLATED/BROKEN.
- Every rejection cites a specific labeled finding — "직감으로 반박" is not a rejection.

## ✅ EXIT CHECK
- [ ] Bear case written at full strength before any rebuttal.
- [ ] Each bear item explicitly rejected (with cited evidence) or upheld (and carried into the verdict).
- [ ] ≥1 refutation thinking-card applied; no thesis built *by* a card.
